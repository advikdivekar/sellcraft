# First-user session: a cosmetic surgeon who sells skincare products

**Who:** a small cosmetic surgeon who sells her own products. Today she makes images in ChatGPT and writes a different caption
for each platform in Claude or ChatGPT, using a different tool or tab for each deliverable.

**Her problems, in her words (fill in exactly what she says):**
- Her information goes to the AI providers: ______
- Safety / content limits on generated images: ______
- Image-generation limits (ran out): ______
- Needs a different tool for every deliverable: ______

**What SellCraft claims to fix:** one place for photos, deck, video and copy for every platform; her facts and photos stay on the
computer running the app; no usage limits; copy that does not invent claims about her product; medical and cosmetic claims that
she did not state are flagged for review.

## Before she tries it
- [ ] Use **product photos only. No patient photos, no before/after photos.** (The phone tunnel passes traffic through Cloudflare,
      so the app should be run on a computer at her clinic or home for anything sensitive.)
- [ ] Pick 3 of her real products and the platforms she actually posts to.
- [ ] Time her current process for one product first (minutes, tools opened): ______
- [ ] Ask permission to quote her and to show screenshots (with product details she is happy to share): ______

## What she does (10-15 min, let her drive; don't help unless she's stuck)
1. Add photos of one product (and note what she does when stuck).
2. Fill in facts. Write down which fields she skips and what she types as "words to avoid".
3. Pick platform, tone, style. Generate.
4. Edit the copy. Note what she changes (this is what the model got wrong for her).
5. Share to her phone / download the kit.
6. Repeat once for a second product.

## Observe and write down
- Time from "Generate" to something she would actually post: ______
- Did the copy invent anything about the product? Did the "Regulated claim" / "Not in your facts" flags show? Did she agree with them?
- Which output did she use first: copy, photo, deck, video?
- Where she hesitated or asked "what does this do?": ______
- Her reaction to the photos (do they look like her real product?): ______

## Ask afterwards (the challenge asks: did they pay, would they, what did they ask for next?)
1. Compared to your current way, what is better? What is worse?
2. Would you use this for your next product launch? Why or why not?
3. Would you pay for it? Roughly how much per month, or per product? (Don't suggest a number first.)
4. What would you ask me to add next?
5. How do you feel about your product details and photos staying on your own computer?

## Honest limits to tell her up front
- It makes images from **her real photos**; it does not invent a product image from a text prompt. (That keeps the product accurate.)
- It flags risky or regulated claims. **It is not legal or regulatory advice.**
- Everything else it can't do yet: voiceover, direct posting to Instagram/Facebook.

## After the session
- [ ] Screenshots of her results (with her permission).
- [ ] Her quotes, verbatim, with the context.
- [ ] Anything she asked for. These go straight into the "what I'd build next" section of the post.
- [ ] Do not invent or polish her reaction; if she was lukewarm, say so.
